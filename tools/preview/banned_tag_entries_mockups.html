Tag entries on the BANNED tab

Today a datapack tag such as #bsp:sanctuary_bans takes one slot on the admin BANNED tab, marked "#" in its corner with the tag's first item as its picture. Its 1,473 items appear nowhere on the screen, and the block browser on the right doesn't mark them either, because it only frames items banned by their own id.

Three ways to show what a tag (or a BAN WHOLE MOD entry) covers. Every option also marks covered items in the browser, so dragging in something the tag already bans shows it is banned. Example region: a Sanctuary plot with the tag on PLACE and USE, plus TNT on its own. Coloured cubes stand in for item pictures.

A. Open the tag click the tile to look inside

The left grid stays as it is: one tile per entry, the tag tile now wide with its name, count and OPEN (as in C's left grid) so it can't be missed. Click it and the left grid shows the tag's items (read-only, dashed frames) under a header with BACK. The tag is one entry, so its rules and REMOVE apply to the whole tag. Items inside it can't be freed one by one; the card says so and names the datapack.

B. Opened in place every item listed, grouped by entry

The left grid lists the region's own single items first, then a heading row for each tag or whole mod with its count and rules, followed by its items, like the players' BANNED HERE. The ▾ folds a group away. Rules and REMOVE on the heading apply to the whole tag. One long scroll for 1,473 items; search narrows it.

C. A BANNED tab in the browser the left grid stays compact

The left grid keeps one wide tile per tag. The block browser gets a new first tab, BANNED, listing every item this region bans, from its own entries, its tags and whole mods. Pointing at an item shows which entry bans it. Everywhere in the browser, items covered by a tag have a dashed frame in the rule's colour and a "#" corner.

Side by side

A. Open the tag

Smallest change; the grid stays one tile per entry, which matches how the flag is stored. Seeing the items takes a click.

B. Opened in place

Everything is visible at once, as players see it. With a big tag the left grid becomes a long list, and the region's own items can scroll away under it.

C. BANNED tab

Keeps the editing side tidy and puts the full list where the blocks are browsed. Best for checking "is this banned?", but the list sits on the other side of the screen from the entries.

Questions

  1. Which option? Default: A, because it's the smallest change and still makes the tag obvious.
  2. Marking covered items in the browser (dashed frame and "#"), for every option? Default: yes.
  3. Adding a tag from the screen: a small field under the grid to type #bsp:sanctuary_bans and press Enter, instead of /rg flag only? Default: yes, on the bar beside BAN WHOLE MOD.
  4. Bans from a parent or the world default shown greyed on the left, with the region they come from (read-only)? Default: no for now. Your plots carry the tag themselves.
