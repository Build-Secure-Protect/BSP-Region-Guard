Wiping claims from the admin screen: three options

Today a world's claims are wiped with /rg claims wipe (owner decision D23): it shows how many claims would go and a one-time four-digit code; /rg claims wipe <code> within a minute writes a backup file, removes every claim in that world (plots for sale go back on sale, outlined plots disappear) and tells every admin online. /rg claims backups and /rg claims restore <file> undo it. Only BSP Core admins may. Each option puts the same steps on screen, keeps the typed code, and shows the backups next to the button. Shown at the moment that matters: the code has been given and is waiting to be typed. Example: the Overworld, 12 claims by 9 players.

A. CLAIMS tab on the world default, the claims listed above the button

The world default gets a CLAIMS tab (ACCESS goes from the world default instead: owners and members of the whole world mean nothing). It lists every claim in this world with owner and price, then WIPE ALL CLAIMS IN THIS WORLD. Pressing it turns the bottom of the tab into the confirmation: the code in large figures, a box to type it, the seconds left, CANCEL. Backups sit at the foot with RESTORE.

Good at: the admin sees exactly what goes before pressing; everything about claims in one place; no extra screen. Weak at: a red button on a tab admins open for other reasons; a long claim list pushes the backups down (scroll).

B. Danger box and its own window a small red box on INFO opens a separate confirmation

The world default's INFO tab ends with a small red box: "Claims in this world: 12. WIPE..." Pressing it opens its own red-framed window over the screen (shown here): what will happen, the code to type, the countdown, WIPE and CANCEL, and the backups with RESTORE underneath. Nothing about wiping is visible until asked for.

Good at: hard to press by accident (two windows deep, red all the way); the confirmation has the whole screen's attention. Weak at: the claims themselves are not listed (only their number); restoring needs going through the wipe window.

C. From the list the CLAIM filter shows the claims and a wipe button under them

Picking the CLAIM filter above the region list shows only claims; a red "WIPE THESE 12" appears where + NEW and WAND usually are. Pressing it turns the right pane into the confirmation (code, box, countdown, CANCEL) with the backups below. The wipe sits right next to the list of what it removes.

Good at: what goes is the list on the left, so there is no doubt; no new tab. Weak at: it reads as "wipe what the filter shows", but the wipe always takes every claim in the world, even ones a search hides; the button moves around with the filter.
