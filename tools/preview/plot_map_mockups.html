PLOT MAP

Three layouts for the new full-window plot map (D57 to D64). Each one below is live: drag the empty map to pan, scroll to zoom (up to 16 pixels a block, with a block grid), pick a plot in the list or on the map, then COPY it and drag the copy into place, or UNLOCK it and move it. A copy or a moved plot turns red where it would partly overlap another, and green when it is clear or sits wholly inside a plot of lower priority.

A. List on the left a slim panel, a bar under the map while placing

How it works

The map fills the window. A slim list on the left has search, type filters and every plot; clicking one snaps the map to it. The picked plot's actions (COPY, UNLOCK) sit at the foot of the list. While placing, a bar along the bottom holds the name, priority, height, ROTATE, CONFIRM and CANCEL.

Good for

Finding plots fast: the list is always there. The map keeps almost the whole window.

Watch out

The bar covers a strip of the map's foot while placing.

B. Floating cards the map is everything, cards float over it

How it works

The whole window is map. The plot list is a card in the top-left corner that folds away; the picked plot gets a small card beside it on the map with COPY and the padlock. While placing, a toolbar follows the copy.

Good for

The most map. Controls appear where you are looking.

Watch out

Cards can sit over the spot you want to see; fold the list or drag the map.

C. Inspector on the right list and details in one column

How it works

The map takes about three quarters of the window. A column on the right lists the plots above and shows the picked plot's details below: size, priority, flags and banned blocks counted, owner, lock. Placement controls appear in the same column.

Good for

Seeing what a plot carries before copying it, and reading every number while placing.

Watch out

A narrower map than A or B.

To decide

  1. Which layout: A, B or C, or a mix. default A
  2. Placing a copy: it follows the mouse until you click to drop it, then you can keep dragging it before CONFIRM. default yes
  3. Snapping: plots move a whole block at a time, and hold SHIFT to line a copy's edge up with a neighbour's. default yes
  4. Other worlds: a world switch at the top shows another world read-only (a gold frame, as the server switcher does). default yes
  5. Undo: after CONFIRM, an UNDO button for ten seconds puts the plot back. default yes

Already decided: a copy carries size, height range, flags, banned blocks, the buyer's allowed flags, price, messages, triggers and priority, never owners or members, and becomes a new plot for sale with a new name; plots are locked until unlocked; a claim someone owns asks before it moves; partial overlaps are refused, a plot wholly inside another needs a higher priority; only the world you stand in can be edited.
