OFFICE PLOTS

Admins choose which plots a Land Office lists. An office with no choice lists every plot for sale in its world, as today.

Opening it: sneak and right-click a Land Office with the Region Wand. Region Guard opens OFFICE PLOTS for that office. Players still open the office as before. Example world: a Sanctuary map with three plot rows and two offices (amber squares on the maps).

A. Tick plots a list of every plot, a map beside it

Every plot for sale and open area in the world, with a tick box. Ticked plots light up on the map. Click a plot on the map to tick it too. The mode switch at the top picks between "every plot" and "only ticked".

For

Exactly what was asked: any mix of plots, one by one. Easy to see what an office lists.

Against

Plots added later by hand (not copied) must be ticked at each office.

B. By area an office lists the plots inside chosen regions

Pick one or more regions (an area, a district). The office lists every plot for sale inside them, including plots made there later. A short list below adds single plots outside those areas.

C. Sale groups plots get a group name, offices list groups

Each plot gets a group on its SALE tab or map card (for example "market", "harbour", "vip"). An office lists the groups ticked here. One plot can be in one group.

What players see

The Land Office looks the same. Its map and list hold only the chosen plots. A player's own claims (to enlarge or give up) and the claims next to an outline still show. A plot that is not listed here can't be bought at this office.

Questions default in brackets

  1. Which layout? [A, tick plots.]
  2. Where the choice is kept: Region Guard keeps it per office (world and position) and gives the Land Office a filtered list; BSP-Core passes the office's position to that call (a small request, as before). [Yes.]
  3. How admins open it: sneak and right-click the office with the Region Wand. [Yes. Also a list of offices on the region map? Default no, later if wanted.]
  4. Open areas (outline your own plot) can be chosen like plots. [Yes.]
  5. Still shown at every office: the player's own claims and claims near an outline. [Yes.]
  6. Buying a plot that is not listed at that office (for example with an old command) is refused there; /claim buy elsewhere is unchanged. [Yes.]
  7. Copies: a plot copied on the region map joins the original's offices. A renamed plot (new id) stays chosen. [Yes.]
  8. A Land Office opened by the wand outline (no office block): lists every plot, as today. [Yes.]
  9. A broken office forgets its choice. [Yes.]
