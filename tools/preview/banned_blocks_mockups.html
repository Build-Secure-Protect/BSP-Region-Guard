BANNED BLOCKS

Three layouts for a new BANNED BLOCKS tab in Region Guard's admin screen, and the window a player sees when they try to walk in carrying something banned. Each screen below works: drag blocks from the browser on the right into the list on the left, and click a banned block's badge to change its rule.

PLACE: can't be placed here USE: can't be right-clicked here BREAK: can't be broken here CARRY: not allowed in the region at all

CARRY covers the other three. A newly dragged block starts with PLACE on. The rules apply to everyone, owners and members included; only admins with bypass on are exempt, and only admins can edit the list.

A. A tab in the pane region list stays on the left

How it works

BANNED joins the tabs. The pane splits: the banned list on the left, the block browser on the right. Click a banned block to pick it; its four switches show underneath.

Good for

Staying inside the screen you know. You can hop between regions on the left without leaving the tab.

Watch out

The tightest of the three: about 6 x 5 banned blocks and 7 x 5 browser blocks show at once, the rest scroll.

B. A full-width editor one row per banned block

How it works

Opening BANNED BLOCKS uses the whole screen. The left side lists each banned block on its own row, with its name, its mod and the four switches. The browser on the right has creative-style tabs down its side.

Good for

Reading the rules at a glance: every block's switches are visible without clicking. Easy to check a long list.

Watch out

Fewer banned blocks show at once (about 9 rows). BACK returns to the region screen.

C. Inventory style pick a rule, then drop blocks

How it works

Looks like Minecraft's own creative inventory: a big chest-like grid on the left, the browser with tabs across the top on the right. Choose a rule on the bar first; every block you drop gets that rule. Right-click a slot to change it.

Good for

Banning lots of blocks quickly with the same rule, and the closest match to "an unlimited inventory" as described.

Watch out

A slot shows its rule only by its border colour and badge; hover for the full card.

The player's side same for every option

Walking in with a CARRY block

They are pushed back a step and this window opens, showing every banned thing they carry, including inside shulker boxes, bundles, backpacks and Curios slots. They aren't asked again until they step away and come back.

Placing a PLACE block

It's refused and stays in their inventory, with a line above the hotbar: "TNT can't be placed in spawn." They may still store it in chests inside.

Containers and the ender chest

Inside the region, chests and other containers won't accept a CARRY block from a player, and the ender chest won't hand one out.

To decide

  1. Which layout: A, B or C, or a mix. default B (the rules are visible without clicking).
  2. Whole mod at once: the BAN WHOLE MOD button bans every block of the hovered block's mod as one entry (shown as the mod's name with a star). default yes
  3. Search: the browser's search matches names and mod ids (create: lists every Create block). default yes
  4. Items as well as blocks: the browser shows items too (lava buckets, end crystals, flint and steel), since CARRY and USE matter for them. default yes
  5. Removing: drag a banned block back to the browser, or press x on it. default both

Already decided (owner, 2026-10-18): per-block rules with four switches; CARRY checks inventory, armour, off-hand, Curios and anything packed in carried containers; inside the region the ender chest won't hand out banned items and containers won't take them; applies to everyone but admins with bypass; only admins edit it; tags by command only.
