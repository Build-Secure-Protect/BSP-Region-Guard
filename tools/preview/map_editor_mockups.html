REGION MAP

Three layouts for the one map that edits everything (D82 to D90). REGION, PLOT and MINE mode buttons sit at the top. Each mode shows its own kind of region strongly and dims the rest. Every mode has the same three tools, PICK, DRAW and RESHAPE, plus its own extras.

Each option shows two windows. The first is REGION mode, reshaping market_hall with its eight handles and its card. The second is MINE mode just after a box was drawn, with the card that creates the new mine. Holding SPACE pans in every mode, so you can click one corner, pan with SPACE and click the second. The gold tags point at what differs between the options.

A · One top bar modes, tools and world in a line

A single bar runs across the top: the modes on the left, the tools in the middle, and the world, UNDO and REDO on the right. The REGIONS list card sits under it on the left and folds. The picked region's card floats beside the region, as on today's plot map.

Questions with these mock-ups

  1. Which layout? Default A: it changes least from the plot map you have tested, and the modes, tools and UNDO stay in one line, so nothing overlaps.
  2. The admin screen's MAP tab: I read your answer as "one map", so the small MAP tab goes and a MAP button (where PLOT MAP is now) opens this map on the selected region. Default: yes. The alternative is to keep the small tab as a preview.
  3. PLOT mode's DRAW: Default: it makes a plot for sale, and the card also asks for the price. Making an open area for players to outline their own plots stays a type change on the card (OPEN).
  4. MINE mode's extras: Default: RESET NOW and SHELL on the bar. The fill table stays in the admin screen's MINE tab, which the card opens.
  5. What dims: Default: in PLOT and MINE modes the other kinds of region are dimmed and can't be picked. REGION mode shows and picks everything.
