BANNED HERE

Two ways for players to see a region's banned blocks and items: a BANNED button in the inventory while they stand where bans apply, and the list on the buying screens. Three options for the list itself. Everything is read-only for players.

Example data: a Sanctuary plot with #bsp:sanctuary_bans on deny-place and deny-use (1,473 entries). Tags are opened into their items on the player's side, so they see real items, not a "#" tile. Coloured squares stand in for item pictures.

The inventory button

Placed each frame to the right of BSP-Core's TEAM and KITS buttons, in the same style, so the three read as one row and never overlap. BSP-Core steps its own buttons past any widget, so there is no tug of war. Shown only while you stand somewhere with bans that apply to you. Also on the creative inventory.

When buying

Before buying (needs BSP-Core): the Land Office is BSP-Core's screen. It would get a BANNED key under BUY that opens Region Guard's list for the picked plot, then returns to the Land Office. Region Guard adds the method; BSP-Core adds the key (a request in the vault, as before).

After buying (Region Guard alone): the "you bought it" pop-up gets a line with the count and a BANNED HERE button.

Questions default in brackets

  1. Which list layout, and which button look? [B for the list, A for the button.]
  2. Before buying: ask BSP-Core for the BANNED key and line on the Land Office? [Yes. Region Guard adds the open-the-list method; the request goes in the vault.]
  3. After buying: add the count line and BANNED HERE button to the bought pop-up? [Yes.]
  4. When is the inventory button shown? [Whenever bans apply where you stand, including the world default. Admins with bypass on see it too, so they can check.]
  5. Tags: open them into their items for players? [Yes. Players see items, not "#".]
  6. Client setting to hide the button (like BSP-Core's teamButton)? [Yes, bannedButton = true.]
  7. Also a command, /claim banned, that opens the list where you stand? [Yes.]
