BANNED BLOCKS: FOUR RULES PER BLOCK
Each banned block or item carries its own rules. They apply to everyone in the region, owners and members too; only admins with bypass on pass.

PLACE

deny-place

The block can't be placed. It stays in the player's hand; they may still store it in a chest inside. Banned buckets can't be emptied.

USE

deny-use

The block can't be right-clicked, and a banned item does nothing in hand: flint and steel, end crystals, a machine's screen.

BREAK

deny-break

The block can't be broken, so a decoration or a machine stays where the admin put it.

CARRY

deny-carry

Not allowed in at all. Carriers are turned back at the edge with a window showing what they hold, even inside shulker boxes, bundles, backpacks and Curios slots. Nothing banned can be picked up or taken out of a chest or ender chest inside.

Name a block by its id (minecraft:tnt), a whole mod (@create) or a tag (#minecraft:beds). Bans add up: a block banned by a region, its parent or the world default is banned everywhere beneath, and a smaller region can't lift it. Teleporting in with a CARRY block is refused too.
