BUYING LAND (WITH BSP-CORE)

1 FIND LAND

Admins put plots up for sale (gold boxes) or open an area with a price per block. Press B to see them in the world.

→

2 OUTLINE IT

In an open area, take the plot tool (/claim tool) and click two corners: the Land Office opens by itself. 5 x 5 up to 64 x 64 blocks, full height; plots may touch, never overlap.

→

3 LAND OFFICE

BSP-Core's Land Office opens with your outline drawn on a map, priced, and nearby claims shown. Buy there, paid from your BSP-Core bank in Shatter Pieces.

→

4 IT IS YOURS

/claim gui: add members, switch the rules strangers follow, set messages, TELEPORT home, ENLARGE later, or give it up for half back.

Claims per world follow the player's LuckPerms rank (3 by default; more for higher ranks). Claims are never allowed in PvP worlds, and nothing in a claim may stop a Shatter Totem steal.
Players buy only at a Land Office; admins keep /claim buy and /claim enlarge for support. Plots that come back on sale return to the list.
