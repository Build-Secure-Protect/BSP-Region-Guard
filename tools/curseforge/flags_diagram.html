HOW A RULE IS DECIDED AT ONE SPOT
Regions stack. Region Guard looks at the regions covering the spot, highest priority first; the first one that sets the flag decides.
alex_bakery - claim - priority 3

sets chest-access allow for its members. Does not set pvp.

market_plots - open area - priority 2

sets pvp deny and mob-spawning deny.

__global__ - the world default

flags for the whole world, the world border, edge links. Sets creeper-explosion deny.

Not set anywhere: the parent region, then the world default, then the flag's own default. Equal priority: deny beats allow.
Build flags (break, place, chests, doors...) need no setting: owners and members may, everyone else may not.
