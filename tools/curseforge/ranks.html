WHO MAY ENTER

A region's RANKS tab (flag allowed-ranks) lets only chosen groups in: a VIP lounge, a members-only market.

RANKED MINES

Gate a mine by rank: the iron mine for everyone, the diamond mine from vanguard up.

MORE CLAIMS

Claims per world by rank: default 3, vip 5, vanguard 8 (claims.limitsByRank).

Works with LuckPerms as a Forge mod or as the Bukkit plugin on Mohist. Without BSP-RankUp, give groups by hand in LuckPerms and everything here works the same.
